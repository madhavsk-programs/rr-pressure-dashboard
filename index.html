Who Delivers When It Matters?

Pressure ratings for every eligible player in the SupeRR Selector Season 2 data pack (IPL and T20Is, 2022 to 2026). Companion to the Round 1 methodology report by Madhav Khurana.

Score unit: runs-equivalent per 100 balls above an average player, adjusted for match situation, phase and opposition.
Usual / Under pressure: raw strike rate (batters) or economy (bowlers) on normal balls and on high-pressure balls (top 25% of leverage in each innings). Shift: modelled change under pressure, with a 90% resampling range. Level: usual level plus shift. Conservative score: 10th percentile of the level over 1,000 match-resampled refits; players are ranked on it. The bar shows the 80% range of the level, with the tick at the conservative score. Ranges are resampling ranges around shrunk estimates, not exact confidence intervals. Pressure shifts did not repeat between halves of players' matches and their spread is no larger than chance, so read them as past behaviour, not as a trait.
